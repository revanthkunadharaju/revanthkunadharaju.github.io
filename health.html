A calm, friendly guide to understanding your medical report

1. Share Your Report

Choose how you'd like to share your report — text, an image/photo, or a PDF file (the way doctors usually share reports). Everything stays on your device.

📸

Click or drop your report image here

JPG, PNG or a photo of your lab report. We automatically enhance the image before reading it.

📸 Tips for a clear photo:
  • Place the report on a flat, dark surface in good light (near a window is ideal).
  • Hold the phone directly above the report — not at an angle.
  • Fill the frame with the report; get close enough that text looks large.
  • Tap the screen to focus before taking the picture.
  • Avoid shadows, glare from lights, and blurry hands — rest the phone on something if possible.

📄

Click or drop your PDF report here

Most doctor and lab reports shared as PDFs work perfectly. Multi-page PDFs are supported too.

📄 About PDF reports:
  • Digital PDFs (typed text, downloaded from a lab portal) read instantly and accurately.
  • Scanned PDFs (photos of pages saved as PDF) are read like images — clear scans work best.
  • If a scanned PDF is hard to read, a clear photo of the report via the Image tab often works better.
